# 📖 Book of Life — Habit Tracker & Life OS

An elegant, high-clarity personal operating system designed around foundational habit tracking starting with **Move**, progressive stacking, and Sunday-to-Saturday weekly performance reviews.

Built with a **Nordic Minimalist** aesthetic for high-agency executive focus and low-cognitive-load, zero-guilt momentum.

---

## 🧭 Habit Tracking Architecture

### 1. Foundational Anchor: Start with **Move**
- **Habit #1**: `Move` (Daily movement, workout, or closing active activity rings).
- Establish physical momentum first before adding cognitive load.

### 2. Track Daily & Stack Habits As You Progress
- Interactive **Sunday-to-Saturday day selector** (`Sun, Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat`).
- Check off habits for today or log past days within the week.
- Dynamic **+ Add New Habit** modal with custom category, daily or weekly frequency (e.g. 3x/week workout), and color styling.
- 1-Click **Recommended Habit Stacks** preset shelf (10k Steps, Hydration, Skincare, Meditation, Reading).
- Edit, archive, or remove habits anytime.

### 3. Strict Sunday-Start Weekly Framework
- All week intervals, matrices, calendars, and calculations strictly start on **Sunday** and end on **Saturday**.
- Rolling 28-day (4-week) Sunday-aligned consistency grid.

### 4. Dedicated Weekly Results & Summary Hub
- **Week-by-Week Navigator**: Browse past, present, or upcoming Sunday–Saturday weeks.
- **Weekly Scorecard**: Overall Consistency Rate %, Perfect Days count (100% days), Total Check-ins, Top Performing Anchor Habit.
- **7-Day Sunday-to-Saturday Habit Matrix**: Full interactive weekly grid showing each habit vs days of the week with progress bars and target completion status.
- **Daily Rhythm Bar Chart**: Visual day-by-day scorecards (Sun–Sat) identifying your most consistent days.
- **Weekly Reflections & Wins**: Dedicated reflection journal saved per week.
- **1-Click Shareable Recap**: Copy formatted markdown weekly summary to clipboard.

## 🏛️ Chapters Overview

1. **Cover Overview (Executive Dashboard)**:
   - **Daily Reminders**: The 5 core principles displayed up front.
   - **Today's Habits & Streak**: Progress ring, streak counter, and instant checklist.
   - **Good Choices (vs Not) Tracker**: Real-time tally for conscious decision momentum.
   - **Day-Specific Goals**: One-off daily targets (e.g. 12k steps today) with no ongoing habit pressure.
   - **Today's Focus**: Top 3 high-impact tasks.

2. **Chapter 1: Habits & Daily**:
   - **Integrated Streak Tracker**: Current streak flame 🔥, best streak, and 7-day mini consistency strip (Sun–Sat).
   - **Sunday-to-Saturday Week Strip**: Intuitive day selector (`Sun, Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat`).
   - **Active Habit Stack**: Anchored with **Move**; customizable cadence, bucket, and notes.
   - **Day-Specific Goals & Choices Tracker**: Contextualized for the active date.
   - **Recommended Stacks**: 1-click presets shelf.

3. **Chapter 2: Tasks & Horizon**:
   - **Quick Inline Capture**: What's your next move? Capture instantly.
   - **Top 3 Primary Focus Slots**: ADHD anchor for daily high-leverage execution.
   - **Clean Horizon Grouping**: Today, This Week / Next Up, Later / Backlog.

4. **System: Settings & Data Vault**:
   - Nordic Light & Midnight Slate dark themes.
   - 100% offline-first local persistence.
   - 1-click JSON backup & restore.

```bash
open index.html
```

